# Firely: project summary

Firely rescues 25 years of wildfire history that would be lost when MODIS retires. NASA's MODIS (2000–present) and VIIRS (2012–present) satellites detect fire at different resolutions. When the record switches from one to the other, counts jump in 2012 and fire seems to increase, when only the sensor changed. Firely merges both records into a single VIIRS-equivalent series on an H3 hexagonal grid, calibrated by fire regime or biome and by month. To validate it, we hide one year at a time and estimate it with the model: compared with a fixed correction factor, it cuts the annual error by about 30% in Mexico and Argentina. On top of that series we built a web app and an Android app that show a burn-activity calendar (years × months) for any area. They include fire-season analysis, analog years, return periods, a 1–3 month outlook and a prevention priority index for fire crews. Gotita, our AI assistant, answers in natural language without ever making up numbers. A Telegram bot sends exercise alerts approved by a person. It matters because without a continuous series, real trends cannot be measured and prevention cannot be planned.
